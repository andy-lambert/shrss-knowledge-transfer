# DECISIONS

> Project cecision log.  
> Keep entries short and explicit. Prefer decisions that reduce future ambiguity for agents and humans.

---

## How to use
- Add a new entry whenever a decision is made regarding project artifacts, direction, open questions, or required points of clarification are addressed by human.
- Each entry should end with a concrete **Decision** or remain **Open** with an explicit owner/date target.

---

## Template

```md
## DR-XXXX: <Title>
- **Timestamp (UTC):** 2026-01-06T10:46:00Z
- **Timestamp (Local):** 2026-01-06T05:46:00-05:00
- **Timezone (IANA):** America/New_York

- **Context**:

- **Decision**:

- **Follow-ups**:

---
```

#### Template Rules (Non-Negotiable)
- Timestamp entries MUST ALWAYS include date AND time
