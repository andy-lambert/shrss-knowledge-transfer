## SHRSS Prerequisite to Discussions: Prepare Discussion Candidates

> [!IMPORTANT]
>
> **Critical prerequisite:** All existing tickets + gap analysis items + new requirements in Jira, optimized for review and analysis

We highly recommend creating a new Jira project, *"SHRSS Phase 2 - Optimization and Hardening"* or the like.

For each of the activities below, follow ticket creation guidelines when creating new tickets and reviewing, pruning, and preparing existing tickets to ensure efficiency, accuracy, and thoroughness during the discussions.

> ***Jira ticket creation guidelines:***
>
> 1. *Use **one issue/piece of functionality per ticket** and **one ticket per issue/piece of functionality**, even if it affects multiple pages or other resources. Use **labels** or **tags** in a **standardized consistent manner** to group tickets by component, process, functional area, site, etc.*
>    - ***Good:** Multipe tickets for distinct issues with the carousel component*
>    - ***Bad:** One carousel component ticket with >1 issue*
> 2. *List all author **and** publish URLs in the ticket*
> 3. *Fill in **Environment (Dev, QA, Stage, Prod)**, **Steps**, **Actual vs Expected**, **Business Impact**, and **Workaround**.*
> 4. *Attach at least **one screenshot or short recording**.*
> 5. *Choose **Severity** based on impact and resolution priority (Blocker/Critical/Major/Minor/Trivial).*
>    - ***IMPORTANT:** If all tickets are showstoppers, the categorization loses value and contributes to churn/noise*
> 6. *Add **clear acceptance criteria** bullets in the ticket so we know exactly what to verify when we mark it as fixed.*
